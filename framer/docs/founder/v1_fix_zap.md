## Open (not in this job)
- F10 text flags are shared, not ZAP-only. pic_probe flags `truncated:clip` on every label in every game. The cause: the shared `ts-face-text` (line-clamp, line-height 1.1) has scrollHeight 2-4 px above clientHeight from glyph overflow. POP, CRACK and LASER fail the same way. Some single-line labels render at 14.6 px at 1280 (shared fit). This belongs to the shared bubble standard (16_eos_bubble_face), per the F10 rule; ZAP was not hacked per game.
- With 6 uploads at 390, one picture is `tinyInBubble`. With uploads, the reaction shows through the ring and glow only, because the user's own picture is kept, as F8 requires.
