ThinkStill Release Console

The integrated Release with the new check-in, game matching, guide arrows, readable text, gathering dots, calm colour shift, shift meter, rewards, safety card and the four new hero games: BIG SIGH (panic), COOL THE VOLCANO (anger), GROUND CONTROL (anxiety) and SKY LANTERNS (sad / lonely). Best on your phone in portrait.

Known issues in this preview

  • Some original games still get stuck. UNFOLLOW stalls at about 17% on phone; the audit found up to 24 games that freeze or never show their ending. Fixing all of them is the next task.
  • Final review fixes are still landing for the arrows, shift meter, rewards, safety card and existing-game fixes, so you may see overlaps or rough edges there.
  • The original 110 games are not premium yet. Pilot A (POP, CLEANSE, CRUSH) is not in this preview; it comes for your approval separately.
  • Character images are compressed for this hosted page (256 px instead of 512 px). In Framer they load at full size.
  • Sound starts after your first tap. Use the speaker button in the app to toggle it.
