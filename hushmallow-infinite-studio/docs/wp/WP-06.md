## What was built

**Planner core (`planner/`)**
- `pick.mjs`: the sampler. `planBatch()` and `planSpec()`.
  - Template choice, then the Mallow (primary weight 3, secondary 1), then ToD and duration (15 s takes half the mass).
  - Then the gag: the Mallow's signature gag 40% of the time where it is feasible, with intensity ≤ the template cap.
  - Then metaphor, turn, ritual (must fit the window), camera, weather and dressing (approved realm content only), and copy (lint-clean in context, cooldowns, reservations).
  - Every choice comes from a named stream of the spec seed (`rng.mjs`). Spec seeds are (batch seed, index, attempt).
  - Production plans are constraint-guided: options the posting rules exclude are filtered before sampling. Templates are weighted by their share of hooks not on cooldown. Plans backtrack a bounded, deterministic number of times on window dead-ends.
  - `options.weight(slot, value)` is the hook WP-25 uses to add least-recently-used weights.
  - plan.json lists each spec with its whole-spec `hash` (round 2).
- `capabilities.mjs` with `library/capabilities.json`: gating. Production needs everything approved. Review mode (`--review`) also admits candidate move cells, never candidate packs or presets.
  - The committed file enables only what is buildable today: Noomi, Moonmilk Cove, late night, 15 s, and the 7 T01 moves.
  - Durations are gated per realm: only 15 s loops seamlessly until WP-14 (audit R6).
- `lint-copy.mjs`: every per-string rule of `templates/validate-library.mjs`, plus:
  - the tone/topic blocklist (self-harm, death, illness, body/food, alcohol, drugs, appearance, body image, self-hatred, catastrophe, outcome claims), single words, explicit phrases and a few patterns;
  - Latin-1 only and clean spacing;
  - time words must match the chosen ToD (lines too, with one listed exemption): keyword classes (library `timeWords` plus `policy.lint.timePhrases`, read with clock phrases masked) ∩ explicit clock times (`time-words.mjs`); contradictory time words fit no ToD;
  - every 'hushmallow' lowercase;
  - breath-hold, hold, breathing-count and breathing-instruction patterns, the `countRun` check, and no breath word in a hook or end card;
  - caution terms are errors unless whitelisted for that exact string;
  - no brand, app or song names (plain, inflected, spacing variants, and app names in app contexts);
  - **a hook or end card is one text** (round 3): every phrase rule also reads the rows joined;
  - **hashtags** (round 3): `lintHashtag` searches the same terms inside glued words, with carrier words;
  - reading-time pre-checks on the beat clock, one readable text per copy element;
  - layout pre-checks with Fredoka metrics;
  - set rules: first words, question marks, grief pairing.
- `time-words.mjs` (round 2, rewritten in round 3): pure clock-time parser (whole phrases, spelled minutes, quarter-to, military hours), `maskClockTimes`, and the ToD intersection.
- `composer-bridge.mjs` (round 3): the planner's one seam to the composer. It loads `composer/{compose,validate,cost}.js` when they exist, checks their results, and returns the G-DIR and G-COST gates. `HM_COMPOSER_DIR` overrides the directory (tests use a stub).
- `policy.json` with `policy.mjs`: every planner choice is data with its source, including weights, caps, uniqueness weights, sequencing, cooldowns, cadence, reading-time and layout constants, the lint word lists, the clock-hour table and the export QA requirements.
- `fingerprint.mjs`: sha256 of canonical visible slots plus seed, `specId()` and `sealSpec()`. `canonical.mjs`: key-order-free hashing.
- `integrity.mjs` (round 2): `specHash` (whole spec), plan.json provenance, shape and fingerprint checks, G-SPEC in the spec's real context. Used by approve, export and mark-posted.
- `distance.mjs`: library 3-slot rule, weighted distance ≥ 6.0, and hook token Jaccard ≥ 0.5 counting as the same hook.
- `schedule.mjs`, the channel posting rules:
  - no back-to-back template, Mallow, ritual or gag;
  - the Mallow quota per 10 posts, scaled to the unlocked set (Q1 default);
  - absence streaks counted from the unlock date, plus a rotation-feasibility check (earliest deadline);
  - ≥ 4 feeling families and ≥ 2 positive/mixed posts per 7;
  - template per week (T01 once);
  - the cadence cap;
  - hook (90 days, semantic), line (3 per 30 days) and resolution (1 per 7 days) cooldowns;
  - reservations and brand-closer runs;
  - 14-day distance.
- `ledger.mjs`: six append-only JSONL kinds, validated on write and on read, all-or-nothing per call. A truncated last line refuses appends. Decisions carry `specHash`, `mode`, `internal` and `gspec`.
- `gspec.mjs`: G-SPEC, judging a spec however it was produced. `compile.mjs`: G-SPEC, then G-DIR/G-COST through the composer seam once the composer exists. Until then those gates are recorded as not-run, with an honest top-level `status`/`pass`.
- `export.mjs`: the caption from library entries, hashtags read from `library/brand.json` by export itself (with recorded provenance and the hashtag lint), the QA contract, export blockers, metadata and `--mark-posted`. `decide.mjs`: approve/reject. `coverage.mjs`, `runs.mjs`, `validate-lib.mjs`, `cli-support.mjs`.

## Deviations from the plan, with reasons

1. **`hm compile` is `hm spec compile`, and `hm library coverage` is `hm lib coverage`.** My file ownership is `cli/commands/{lib,plan,spec,approve,reject,export}.mjs`. A top-level `compile.mjs` would collide with the composer package, which must own the real compile. It can be a one-line alias that calls `planner/compile.mjs` and then G-DIR.
2. **G-DIR is not run** (criterion 4, partial). The composer does not exist yet. Round 3 defines the interface the planner expects of it (`compose`, `gdir`, `gcost`; `docs/SPEC.md`), because compile must call it. WP-03 may change the shape by editing only `planner/composer-bridge.mjs`.
3. **Ledger data is git-ignored.** The plan says "committed"; the orchestrator's instruction for this package says git-ignore it.
4. **Reading time: one readable text per copy element, with a cyclic hook window.** Round 1 checked each rendered row of the hook and end card, which the verifier showed can never fail. Round 2 reads the hook and end card as one text each, as an error. The hook's window counts the hook's return at HOOK_IN through the loop seam (`policy.reading.hookWindow: "cyclic"`, a planner default the owner may change to `"first-view"`, which fails 87/128 hooks at 10 s).
5. **The default cadence cap is 14 posts per week, not 21.** The valence rule needs ≥ 2 positive/mixed posts per 7, and the 9 positive/mixed templates hold 72 hooks: under the 90-day hook cooldown that allows at most 252 posts per 90 days (19.6/week). 7. **Tone caps generalise the explicit ones.** `byTone` gives gentle ≤ 0.4 and gentle-comic ≤ 0.6, extending T14's and T09's caps to T18 and T20 (planner default).
8. **More planner modules than the plan lists.** Support modules were added (`integrity`, `time-words` in round 2). WP-25's `weights`, `reserve` and `calibrate` were **not** created.
