# ThinkStill Reflect — Creative Bible

**Reflect is a playground for human perspectives: one situation, several real minds, a reveal that shows how differently
they saw it.** People are the intelligence. No generative AI anywhere in ritual gameplay. Scripted Bubble companions fill
empty seats, always labelled, always driven by seeded rules, never presented as people.

Player-facing language says **ritual**, never game.

## The dramatic spine (each ritual invents its own version)

HOOK (3 s) → PREDICT → PLAY (private, physical) → HUMAN COLLISION → REVEAL → PERSPECTIVE SHIFT → FINALE → REPLAY

## Non-negotiables (from both briefs, binding)

1. **A world, not a room.** Each ritual has its own environment, geometry, camera language, motion vocabulary, sound
   identity and rules. Shared code stops at low-level primitives: 3D projector, camera, particles, audio bus, gestures,
   room transport, Bubble actor. Never a shared stage with different labels.
2. **A verb, not a form.** Each ritual has one unmistakable main verb (scrub and spotlight; time and perform; sculpt and
   ride) and at least three stages: discover → interact/transform → collective reveal. Typing only where integral, and
   always optional.
3. **Characters act.** Bubble characters anticipate, strain, react, recover and celebrate in response to input. Never
   static badges. Circular art, fully visible, never cropped, no black masks; text sits below the image, regular weight.
4. **Film grammar.** Every beat is planned as anticipation → input → physical response → visual consequence → reveal →
   aftermath, with camera moves and foreground/background separation. Effects and sound are triggered by actions, not
   looping decoration.
5. **Distinct finales.** Each ritual's finale visualises how these specific people's views diverged or overlapped, keeps
   the original situation intact, never claims one correct reading where evidence is uncertain, and lets the original
   player choose what resonates and what gets shared.
6. **Instant entry.** The ritual starts before any name form. Solo play uses labelled Bubble companions and a fictional
   scene. Inviting real people is a contextual step, never a wall.
7. **Real multiplayer.** Authoritative room state, server-validated actions, sealed private submissions until the reveal,
   lobby caps, duplicate prevention, rejoin, consent to share, host moderation. Proven with two separate browsers.
8. **One screen of play on a phone.** 390×844 active play fits without scrolling; tested at 360×780, 375×812, 390×844,
   430×932, 1280×860 and 1440×900. 44 px touch targets, touch + pointer, tap alternatives, safe areas, reduced motion,
   mute.
9. **Measured, not claimed.** Input feedback < 100 ms, sound start < 50 ms after the gesture where the platform allows,
   60 fps target; only reported from measurements.
10. **Safe and private.** Fictional scenes by default; personal content optional, sealed, never in share links without
    consent. No ratings of feelings, no scoring distress, no competitive judging of personal experiences, no manipulative
    return mechanics.

## Quality rubric (score 1–10 with evidence; launch needs 9+ on the creative five)

Hook · Originality · Visual quality · Interaction · Social discovery · Surprise · Replayability · Mobile quality.
Automatic rejection: questionnaire, reskin, non-functional control, fake multiplayer, unlabelled automated participant,
text-only reveal, clipped/overlapping UI, unreadable Bubble faces, repeated finale, routine long typing, broken loop.

## Visual language

- **Light first.** Every world has a key light, a colour temperature and a time of day. Night and dusk worlds use warm
  practical lights (string lights, lamps, screens) against cool fill.
- **Depth.** At least three planes: foreground occluders, the action plane, a background that moves with the camera.
- **Bubble cast.** Glitch (logic, detective), Patch (relationships), Drop (meaning, feelings), Rush (urgency, action),
  Still (calm, body), Sync (emotions, impulses), Loopie (loops). Casting follows the corpus's bubble for the source ritual.
- **Typography.** Each world picks a display face from Google Fonts with a system fallback; UI chrome uses the console face.
- **Sound.** Each world has its own instrument palette (synthesized, so nothing to load); Bubble music comes from Reset's
  shared-media bridge when present; every action has a sound inside 50 ms.

## Companion rules

Companions take real seats with real moves chosen by authored, seeded rules (each Bubble has a recognisable bias: Glitch
literal, Loopie dramatic, Rush fast and extreme, Still gentle). They are labelled "Bubble companion" everywhere their
contribution appears, including the reveal and any share image.
