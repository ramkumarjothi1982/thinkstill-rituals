1. What stays on your device

You can use hushmallow without an account and without telling us who you are. What the app keeps is stored in your browser’s local storage on this device, under names that start with hm2:. It is not sent to us, and we can’t see it:

  • your settings (such as sound, motion and text size) and your plan;
  • your favourite worlds, where a Hushflow paused, and which Mallow lines and rituals you’ve already had, so they don’t repeat too often;
  • your Memory Garden: the skills you choose to keep, with when you kept and practised them, never your words or photos;
  • if “Remember what I played” is on: which rituals and worlds you played, the feelings you picked for them and how they felt afterwards;
  • if “Keep sealed predictions” is on: the predictions you sealed in the simpler Another Angle, with your words;
  • if you choose “Keep it on this phone” for a Belief Mallow in Another Angle: its words, the object you picked for it, how far you explored it and the day you kept it (three at most);
  • for Another Angle: your sharing choice, a random code for this device, a session code, any tester code, and a count of the feedback buttons you tapped.

While the app is open, it also keeps a short note for that browser tab only (in session storage, for up to 12 hours), so that after something heavy it stays gentle if the page reloads. The note holds only how the visit should continue, never your words.

Your words and photos. What you type is used on your device to bring your thought to life. It isn’t saved, except as above, and it isn’t sent anywhere unless you choose Another Angle (section 3). Photos you add stay in the app’s memory on this device while you use them: they are never uploaded and never saved.

2. If you create an account

An account keeps your things apart from anyone else’s on a shared device and, only if you choose, brings them to a new one. Our account service is provided by Supabase, and account data is stored in Sydney, Australia (Supabase region ap-southeast-2).

An account is optional. If you make one, your email (and your name and home Mallow, if you choose them) is kept with it, as is when you last opened the app; if you switch on sync, so are the parts you choose: your settings, favourite worlds, Memory Garden and where a Hushflow paused. Never your words, feelings or photos.

  • Your password is handled by the sign-in service and stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. We never see it.
  • We also keep when your account was created, and your sync choices with when you made or withdrew them.
  • Your agreement. To create an account, you tick a box to confirm you’re 18 or older and agree to the Terms and this policy. Your account keeps when you created it with the box ticked, and which versions of the Terms and this policy you agreed to, with its sign-in record.
  • The sign-in service keeps its own security records, such as when you signed in and from which network address, to protect your account. Deleting your account doesn’t delete these records: they are kept for [Owner: how long Supabase keeps its sign-in security records on our plan].

3. Another Angle: AI and sensitive information

Another Angle is part of hushmallow Beyond. It is the only part of hushmallow that sends your thoughts or feelings off your device, and only after you tap Accept on its consent card. If you tap Not now, nothing is sent and Zuzu’s own angles play on your device. As the card says:

Your words go to our secure server, where an AI language model from Anthropic, an outside provider, suggests a perspective.

If that notice changes in a way you should read again, the card asks you again before anything more is sent.

Checked on your device first

  • Before anything is sent, the app reads your words on your device. If they suggest you may be in danger or need support, it shows support lines instead, and nothing is sent.
  • Some words about a loss stay on your device too: Zuzu’s own angles, or a quieter moment, play instead.
  • Before each angle, the app asks whether you’d like another angle or just to be with it. Nothing is sent before you choose, and nothing is sent if you choose Just be with it.
  • Our server checks the words it receives again, before anything goes to Anthropic. If they suggest you need support, it answers with support lines and Anthropic is never asked.

What’s sent

  • For each angle: only the words you choose, the feeling and the kind of help you pick, and (for another angle) the lines you’ve already seen and what kind they were, with the app’s language and a one-off random code.
  • To start: a random code for this device, the app’s version and the type of device (and any tester code), so the daily allowance stays fair. Our server replies with a session code, which the app keeps on your device for up to 30 days and sends with each request.
  • Signed in, with web subscriptions: on the web (not in the App Store app), when you’re signed in, the start also sends your account’s access token, so our server can confirm your account with our account service and check your plan. Like any access token from our account service, it carries your account’s email address and what was recorded with your account when you signed up: your name, if you gave one, and your agreement to the Terms and this policy (section 2). Our server uses only your account’s id from it: it doesn’t log the token or what it carries, and keeps only a one-way scrambled code of it, in memory for up to a minute, so it needn’t ask again. The token is never sent with your words, and never to Anthropic. The daily allowance is then counted for your account.
  • For a feedback tap: which button, which Mallow and what kind of angle it was (and its version), never your words.
  • Never your photos; and Anthropic is never sent your account, its email address or the name on it.

What our server keeps

hushmallow stores nothing you write and doesn’t log your words. Our server runs on Cloudflare (section 5) and keeps only:

  • daily usage counts per device (or per account, with a web subscription) and per network, filed under one-way scrambled codes (never your network address, device code or account itself), deleted two days after the day they count;
  • a short technical record of each request, such as whether it worked and how long it took; codes for the feeling, the kind of help, the area of life it is about (such as work or family) and the kind of perspective; a rough length (short, medium or long); and a coarse safety level (none, concern, care or crisis). It never holds your words, the perspectives you see, your network address or your device code. These records are kept for [Owner: how long Cloudflare keeps the server’s logs on our plan].

Anthropic

Anthropic, PBC, based in the United States, receives your words, the feeling and the kind of help you picked and the earlier lines, with a code that changes every day and doesn’t identify you. It prepares the perspective you see.

Anthropic doesn’t use your words to train its AI. It usually deletes them within 30 days.

If its automatic safety checks flag a message as breaking its rules, Anthropic can keep it for up to 2 years. It also keeps anything the law requires it to.

Anthropic uses your words only to prepare your perspective, to keep its service safe, and where the law requires. It isn’t sent your account or photos, but it sees anything you type, names included.

Sensitive information

What you write may say something about your health, your relationships or other private matters, so we handle it as sensitive information. We send it only with your consent (Accept on the consent card), only to prepare your perspective, and our server doesn’t keep it. You don’t need to include names or details that identify you or anyone else: the perspective works without them.

Changing your mind

In Settings → Privacy, switch off “Share my words with Another Angle” at any time. “Delete my Another Angle data” removes this device’s Another Angle codes, your sharing choice, your feedback, any sealed predictions and any Belief Mallows you kept. Our server keeps no copy of your words, so there is nothing of yours to delete there, and its usage counts are cleared after two days.

Anthropic’s copy is usually deleted within 30 days (kept longer only if flagged or the law requires it). We can’t delete it sooner from here.

4. Payments

Subscriptions to hushmallow Flow and hushmallow Beyond on the web are processed by Stripe. You enter your payment details and email address on Stripe’s secure checkout: we never see or store your card number. Stripe handles the payment information it collects as an independent controller, under its own privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy), and may process it in Australia, the United States and other countries.

To connect a payment to your account, we give Stripe your account’s random reference code, never your name or email. Our server then keeps, for each subscribing account, only what it needs to give you your plan: Stripe’s references for you and your subscription, the plan and billing period, its status, when the period ends and whether it ends then, and since when a payment has been failing; and, for 30 days, the references of Stripe’s updates it has already applied. Never your card details, email address or name. Your device keeps your plan, its status and when it renews.

If you subscribe through an app store instead, that store handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy.

12. Keeping it safe

  • Everything the app sends travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Each account can reach only its own records in our database.
  • Our Another Angle server never stores or logs your words.
  • If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we’ll tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires.

No online service can be perfectly secure, and the device you use matters too. If other people use your device, an account or Delete everything keeps your things your own.

13. Age

hushmallow is for people aged 18 and over. To create an account, you tick a box to confirm you are; we don’t check age in any other way. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone younger. If you think someone under 18 has created an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

14. Not for emergencies

hushmallow is an emotional companion platform. It isn’t a crisis service, therapy or medical care, and it can’t respond to emergencies. If you or someone else is in danger, call 000 in Australia, or your local emergency number elsewhere. If you’re thinking about ending your life, please reach out now:

  • Australia: emergency 000; Lifeline 13 11 14 (any time); Suicide Call Back Service 1300 659 467; 13YARN 13 92 76 (for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people).
  • Elsewhere: your local emergency number (such as 112, 911 or 999), or find a free, confidential line in your country at findahelpline.com. UK and Ireland: Samaritans 116 123. US and Canada: 988.
