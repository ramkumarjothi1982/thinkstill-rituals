    What’s real and what’s simulated
    • Real: the rules (consent, limits, blocking, text rules, rate limits, moderation access) run in the service and are covered by automated tests, including forged requests.
    • Real: each phone talks to the service only through a network layer with latency, offline mode and lost replies.
    • Simulated: the service runs inside this page and saves to this browser. Sign-in is simulated for labelled test identities. No Supabase, no server, no real people.
    • Also seeded: StarMallow and FernMallow (discoverable test identities nobody is operating) and DreamMallow (hidden; never appears in Find).
