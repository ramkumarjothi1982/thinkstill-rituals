# Launch 30: the games ThinkStill launches with

*2026-10-10 · for the founder · picked from all 114 games*

## In one minute

- **We launch with 30 games, not 114.** The other 84 stay in the code, switched off. Any of them can come back by editing one launch list.
- **The goal for every pick:** a stressed person feels lighter within a minute, enjoys it in their hands, and wants to come back because it felt good. There are no streaks, timers or "don't lose your progress" hooks.
- **All four new hero games are in:** BIG SIGH, COOL THE VOLCANO, GROUND CONTROL and SKY LANTERNS. **Both of your showcase fixes are in:** ZAP with the real zapper and UNHOOK with the real hook.
- **Every feeling ThinkStill serves has at least 3 launch games.** Panic, anxiety, anger, overthinking, overwhelm, sad, lonely, shame, fear, jealousy, numb, good and "not sure" (general) are all covered. That gives the no-repeat rotation real variety.
- **Every pick finishes on a phone and on desktop.** A game that has frozen or stalled in testing is out until it's fixed, however good it is. That rule cost us SLINGSHOT (best hand-feel in the arcade) and BURN (most hypnotic flame).
- **Distinct:** no more than 2 games share a look, and no two play the same way. 12 of the 30 have a world of their own, and ZAP and UNHOOK were rebuilt with their own look.
- **CRACK and STOMP are not in.** Their real tools (the spanner and the boot) are still unfinished, and their old version is the "pick a tool, tap each bubble 3 times" loop that ZAP now does much better.
- **The mix of intensity:** 10 big releases (smash, fling, erupt, shred), 10 playful ones (laugh, swipe, snap, sort) and 10 slow, calm ones (breathe, float, drift, quiet).

## How we chose

Two separate reviews scored all 114 games, and neither saw the other's scores.

1. **Launch readiness (evidence).** This used the rater scores from the quality audit, whether the game reliably finishes on a phone (smoke runs, the stall fixes, the all-114 arrow sweep), open founder issues, and how often the router serves the game.
2. **Creative strength.** Would someone screen-record it and share it? Is it absorbing? Does it feel good in the hands? Does it bring relief fast? Is it its own game, not a re-skin?

We combined the two scores, then applied five rules:

- **Reliable on a phone.** If a game can freeze, it's out.
- **Every feeling covered.** At least 3 games per feeling, using the router's own lists.
- **At most 2 games per look-alike group** (the screen templates in the quality report).
- **No two games that play the same way.**
- **A mix of big releases and slow calm.**

## Build notes (for switching the 30 on)

- **ZAP has to be routed.** Today it sits on the router's bench (`EOS_BENCH` in `src/eos/45_eos_router.jsx`), so it is never auto-picked. Take it off the bench and add it to `anger.mid` (after HOT POTATO), `overthinking.mid` and `numb.high`/`numb.mid`. It is already a discharge game, so the existing "never smash at a sad, lonely or shame peak, never for good" rules keep applying.
- **"ONE MORE" for jealousy:** none of jealousy's act-2 games (119, 115, 117) is in the 30, so it should fall back to general (BIG SIGH, POP, RAIN OUT).
- **Optional extra variety (one line each):** add 75 INK BLEED to `shame.mid` (it loosens harsh words, which fits shame) and 41 UNHOOK to `jealous.mid` (unhook from comparison).
- **Thin bands that fall back gracefully:** overwhelm at its peak has only BIG SIGH, then moves to the middle-band games. Fear in the middle band has only X-RAY, then moves to BIG SIGH, FINGER TRAP and GROUND CONTROL.
- **CRACK (3) and STOMP (4) are not in the 30**, so both go back to their classic engine and the unfinished tool work never ships.
- Nothing in the 30 is in the router's vault, and nothing is held from phones (SLINGSHOT, the only phone hold, is out).

## Small fixes worth doing before launch (none blocks the pick)

- **RAIN OUT:** the 4th cloud clips on a phone, and the desktop drag has been flaky in some runs. The washout should remove every word.
- **POP:** the 6th bubble clips at the right edge.
- **SHRED:** the face being fed into the blades should look relieved, not crying.
- **MISS ON PURPOSE:** hitting the target should get a friendly "too perfect!" nudge instead of a silent reset.
- **MUTE, KEEP / DROP, TINY SOUNDTRACK, GO WEIRD and RAIN OUT:** the audit saw them end on a distressed face. They should end happy.
- **TINY SOUNDTRACK:** the keys should work before a vibe is picked, and the first load is slow.
- **X-RAY:** the BURN ALL label.
- **UNFOLLOW:** re-confirm the plug fix on a real phone, because you saw this one stall.

## First to come back (once fixed)

1. **SLINGSHOT (24):** the best hand-feel in the arcade. Fix the stall at phone width.
2. **BURN (18):** the most hypnotic flame. Fix the ~3 s phone freeze and the slow finish.
3. **CLEANSE (109):** your reference game. It returns as its Pilot A rebuild, so it no longer plays like BIG SIGH.
4. **SPACE MAKER (93):** strong for overwhelm, but blocked by the 2-per-look limit with HOT POTATO and GO WEIRD.
5. **VELCRO (44):** a lovely slow peel, but it plays like UNFOLLOW (same panel, same drag right).
