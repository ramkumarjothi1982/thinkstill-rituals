1. What hushmallow is

hushmallow is a wellbeing and entertainment app: playful worlds, rituals and seven Mallows for noticing, expressing and playing with what you feel. Many people enjoy small moments like these, but hushmallow is not therapy, counselling or medical advice.

  • It doesn’t diagnose any condition, and it isn’t a substitute for care from a doctor or another qualified professional.
  • We don’t promise any particular result from using it.
  • If you’re worried about your health or wellbeing, please talk to a doctor or another health professional.

2. Not a crisis service

hushmallow can’t respond to emergencies, and nobody watches what you write in it. If you or someone else is in danger, or you’re thinking about ending your life, please reach out now:

  • Australia: emergency 000; Lifeline 13 11 14 (any time); Suicide Call Back Service 1300 659 467; 13YARN 13 92 76 (for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people).
  • Elsewhere: your local emergency number (such as 112, 911 or 999), or find a free, confidential line in your country at findahelpline.com. UK and Ireland: Samaritans 116 123. US and Canada: 988.

Support lines are also one tap away in the app, free, whatever your plan.
