## How to read this register

**Classification** (the column that matters most). Recommendations are never presented as obligations.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Verified** | Verified obligation: the law was seen (as a search extract) and it binds Hushmallow on the known facts |
| **Depends** | Depends on Hushmallow's functionality or business circumstances. The row says which fact |
| **Advice** | Needs legal advice: a judgement, or a text nobody here has seen |
| **Practice** | Recommended practice: guidance or good practice, not a legal duty |
| **N/A (checked)** | Not applicable on the facts: the test was applied and the facts don't meet it. The row says what would change that |

**Source: how it was seen.** No official site could be opened from this environment. Every source was seen through a
search tool, which sometimes paraphrased the page. A secondary source is never cited as the law.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| text (extract) | Official legislation text, seen only as a search extract |
| guidance (extract) | An official regulator's or government page, seen only as a search extract (sometimes paraphrased) |
| earlier extract | Seen in an earlier research run's extract, not re-seen on 15 October |
| title only | Only the title or heading was seen |
| secondary | A secondary source, used only as a lead or for a fact such as a date; not the law |
| memory | From the researcher's memory; **not seen**. Check before relying on it |
| not law | Guidance from a non-government or overseas body (Mindframe, Orygen, Samaritans, Beyond Blue): practice only |

**Owner** (who acts): **Owner** = Hushmallow's owner (decisions, business facts, approvals, publishing). **Developer** =
code and document changes. **Safety lead** = the desk procedure (named in `docs/ESCALATION.md`, not yet appointed).
**Lawyer** = an Australian lawyer (decision D7). **Tier A** = the independent safety reviewer (`docs/REVIEWS.md`). The
owner is accountable for every row.

**Covered in** names the draft. Unless a path is given, it is in `docs/legal/`: `PRIVACY_POLICY`, `TERMS_OF_USE`,
`COLLECTION_NOTICE_AND_CONSENT`, `COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES`, `SAFETY_DISCLAIMERS`, `MODERATION`, `RETENTION_AND_DELETION`,
`DATA_BREACH_RESPONSE_PLAN`, `AGE_ELIGIBILITY`. **[GAP]** means no draft covers it yet.

**The critic's corrections** (they override the research items they name): TGA-2 (not an obligation, an interpretive
rule; add the public repository), TGA-3 (add the alleviation limb), TGA-4 and TGA-5 (needs legal advice; the published
documents; the selection logic), TGA-11 (add the planned classifier), U16-02 (drop the catcher's count), U16-05 (not

one position on response times), SCW-12 (the designated-message categories), SCW-13 (only the duty is verified),
SM-01 (review lines as replies to undetected risk), SM-12 (the desk can't show support to an author later).

A row's first label is the one counted. Where a row has two parts (U16-12, SCW-13, TGA-2), the row explains both.

### Four questions decide most of this register

1. **Is Hushmallow an APP entity?** (PRV-01, PRV-02.) Turnover is far below A$3m, so it is exempt unless an exception
   applies. The live exception is "provides a health service ... and holds any health information" (s6D(4)(b)). Every
   APP row, the data breach scheme and the Children's Online Privacy Code turn on it. **Needs legal advice.** Until
   then, every draft is written as if Hushmallow is bound (PRV-03).
2. **Is Hushmates an age-restricted social media platform?** (U16-01 to U16-03.) The base test leans met, the 2026
   condition leans met through time-limited availability, and the only realistic exclusion (health purpose) cuts against
   the non-clinical positioning. **Needs legal advice before a public launch** (D9). See `AGE_ELIGIBILITY.md`.
3. **What is Hushmates' intended purpose, for the TGA?** (TGA-1, TGA-2R, TGA-4, TGA-5.) Not a medical device on the
   in-app copy, but the public repository publishes "crisis detection" and CBT, DBT and ACT framing, and Mallow lines are
   chosen by analysing the author's words for thinking patterns. **Needs legal advice.** Making the repository private
   is the cheapest risk reduction (D19).
4. **Which Online Safety Act category, and which risk tier?** (SCW-01, SCW-02.) It leans "social media service",
   probably Tier 3. That decides which code measures are mandatory. **Needs legal advice**, and a written risk
   assessment either way.

### Things that bind now, whatever those answers are

- **The Disability Discrimination Act** duty not to discriminate in providing the service, free beta included (SCW-13).
- **The Australian Consumer Law** ban on misleading conduct, for every word in the app, the drafts and any marketing
  (SCW-07). Whether something breaches it depends on the words; the ban itself applies.
- **The statutory privacy tort**, for intentional or reckless misuse, whatever the APP answer (PRV-20).
- **Some work health and safety duty**, to desk readers or to the owner as a self-employed person (SCW-15).
- **eSafety notices**, the moment one arrives (SCW-06).
