1. What hushmallow is

hushmallow is an emotional companion platform: playful worlds, rituals and seven Mallows for noticing, expressing and playing with what you feel. Many people enjoy small moments like these, but hushmallow is not therapy, counselling or medical advice, and it isn’t medical treatment or medical care.

  • It doesn’t diagnose any condition, and it isn’t a substitute for care from a doctor or another qualified professional.
  • We don’t promise any particular result from using it.
  • If you’re worried about your health or wellbeing, please talk to a doctor or another health professional.

2. Not for emergencies

hushmallow is a companion, not therapy or medical care. It isn’t a crisis service: it can’t respond to emergencies, and nobody watches what you write in it. If you or someone else is in danger, call emergency services now: 000 in Australia, or your local emergency number elsewhere (such as 112, 911 or 999). If you’re thinking about ending your life, or need someone to talk to now, please reach out:

  • Australia: emergency 000; Lifeline 13 11 14 (any time); Suicide Call Back Service 1300 659 467; 13YARN 13 92 76 (for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people).
  • Elsewhere: your local emergency number (such as 112, 911 or 999), or find a free, confidential line in your country at findahelpline.com. UK and Ireland: Samaritans 116 123. US and Canada: 988.

Support lines are also one tap away in the app, free, whatever your plan. The app shows the lines for the region your phone is set to first.

9. Another Angle and AI

  • Another Angle is part of hushmallow Beyond. It sends your words only after you say yes on its consent card, and you can switch it off at any time in Settings → Privacy. Our Privacy Policy explains what is sent, to whom, and what is kept.
  • Its perspectives are prepared automatically by an AI language model from Anthropic, an outside provider. They are suggestions to consider, not advice and not facts about you or your situation. They may be wrong, incomplete or not right for you.
  • You decide what to make of them. Please don’t rely on them for decisions about your health, safety, money or legal matters.
  • If what you write suggests you may need support, the app may offer support lines instead of a perspective. When the app’s own check on your device does this, nothing is sent. Our server checks again, and the AI model can also answer with support lines: by then your words have been sent (to our server, and in the model’s case to Anthropic), as our Privacy Policy explains. These checks are automatic and can be mistaken or miss things, so please don’t rely on them to keep you safe.
  • Another Angle has a fair daily allowance and may sometimes be unavailable. When it is, Zuzu’s own angles play instead.
