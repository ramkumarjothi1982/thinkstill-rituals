## What was built

### The QA runner: `qa/run-qa.mjs` and `hm qa` (`cli/commands/qa.mjs`)

**What it does with one target**, a video plus whatever QA can know about it:

1. **Plan and compile gates:** G-SPEC, G-DIR and G-COST run on what was rendered.
2. **Direction geometry:** `qa/gates/geometry.mjs`.
3. **Brand probes:** `qa/gates/probes.mjs`.
4. **Three jobs in parallel:**
   - one decode of the video through every pixel gate (`qa/gates/pixels.py`);
   - the audio gate (`qa/gates/audio.py`);
   - the determinism re-render (`qa/gates/determinism.mjs`).
5. **The combining gates.**
6. **`report.json`** (`hm-qa/1`). The report meets the QA contract that `hm export` enforces (`planner/export.mjs qaProblems`): `video.sha256`, `spec.fingerprint`, 18 gates with boolean `pass`, and `G-FINAL:*` plus `G-AUDIO` present.

**Targets:**

- `v2`;
- a farm job or variant directory;
- a batch directory;
- an `hm-qa-target/1` manifest;
- a bare MP4.

**Stages:**

- `final`: all 18 gates;
- `draft`: 7 gates, namely G-SPEC, G-DIR, G-COST, the three G-DRAFT gates and the brand mark.

**Subcommands:**

- `hm qa fixtures` runs the fixture suite;
- `hm qa gallery` builds the review gallery.

## Deviations from the plan, with reasons

1. **Gate files.** The plan lists `qa/gates/{spec,direction,cost,draft,...}`. G-SPEC, G-DIR and G-COST share `spec-gates.mjs`, because they share the planning context and G-SPEC decides whether the others run. G-DRAFT is split into `safezone`, `readability` and `layout`, and brand marks span `marks.py` and `brand-marks.mjs`. The report names gates as `<group>:<part>` (`G-FINAL:flash`, …), which the export contract accepts.
2. **One gate can own several fixtures:** flash, fidelity, logo, G-AUDIO, G-SPEC and brand marks each have two. "Exactly its own gate" is judged as "exactly one failed gate, the expected one", and the expected check must fail inside it.
3. **No rest probes.** The plan's identity-post, Mallow-only rest probes need an engine probe mode that `engine/` does not have, and `engine/` is not WP-05's. G-FINAL:fidelity judges the delivered pixels instead, with four checks: a grade guard on sample points that the soft-body mesh places on screen, skin sharpness against the pristine art, the rest-fade spike, and the magnification log. WP-10 should add the probe mode.
4. **Logo reference.** The probe is the engine's `LogoCard` drawn on a canvas, not rendered "with the same post-processing". The video's crop is averaged over the full-alpha frames to remove the time-seeded grain. v2 measures SSIM 0.988 (a single frame 0.981), so the limit is a provisional 0.95, with 0.995 as the warn band.
5. **Readability uses p90 for light text.** For light text the conservative end of the background ring is its bright end; dark ink uses p10, as in the plan.
6. **Calibrated limits where v2 sits outside the plan.** They are listed under criterion 4. Most notable is the legacy audio seam step: the frozen score's R7 wrap step is −31.3 dBFS, so it gets a −24 dBFS limit. Every other score must meet −60 dBFS.
7. **The approved v2 is judged as its production twin.** The golden spec is a review (INTERNAL) spec, the composer's parity spec, and judging it would require a mark. The twin has the same visible slots, seed, fingerprint and id. The render evidence stands in for `provenance.json`, which the legacy route never wrote, and it is tied to the approved frames by the frame-hash chain.
8. **Realm references come from v2** (provisional) until WP-19's app captures. v2 therefore scores about 0 on realm likeness by construction. The re-encoded fixtures score 0.35–0.43 and the WP-02 final profile 0.04–0.05.
9. **Fixture videos are re-encodes** at x264 CRF 14 preset medium, so they carry one more encoder generation than the approved master. Each fixture's render evidence is pinned to its own video, because a fixture stands for a render whose provenance is in order. The re-encode shifts the measurements a little: logo SSIM 0.985 against 0.988; sharpness 0.863 against 0.835.
10. **Fixture runs skip the determinism re-render by default.** The re-render never reads a fixture's pixels; `--render` includes it, and the acceptance run used `--render`.
11. **G-DUPES reads signatures** from ledger `fingerprints.jsonl` records that carry `signature`, and from corpus files. WP-25 writes the ledger records from `report.signature`.
