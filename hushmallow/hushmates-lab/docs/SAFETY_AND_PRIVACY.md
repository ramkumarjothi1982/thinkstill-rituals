# Hushmates safety and privacy: working draft

This is product and engineering intent for review. It is **not** a legal
privacy policy and needs legal review before any pilot.

## Pilot conditions (before anyone real uses this)

- **Adults only, invitation-only pilot.** A self-declared birthday doesn't
  prove adulthood, and age assurance isn't implemented. Access must stay
  controlled by invitation until it is.
- **No stranger discovery without moderation capacity.** If people on the
  safety team can't review reports within the stated times, keep Find a
  Hushmate off and allow private codes only.
- **Escalation route** for urgent categories (exploitation, suspected minor,
  safety concern): a named on-call person, guidance for contacting
  authorities where the law requires it, and an incident log.

## Community standards (shown in Safety & help)

Be kind. No harassment, hate or threats. No sexual content, and nothing
involving anyone under 18. No grooming, exploitation or manipulation. Never
ask for money, gifts or payment details. Don't pressure anyone to share
contact details or move to another app. Respect "no", quiet mode, breaks and
goodbyes.

## Deterministic protections (no AI)

- Consent before any conversation exists. Limits on Hushmates, pending
  invitations and invitations per day.
- 30-day quiet period after a decline or disconnect. Blocks are enforced in
  the service and protect against re-contact.
- Text rules for links, contact details, outside usernames and money
  requests. These are **imperfect by design**, and the interface says so.
- Rate limits on messages, discovery, code guesses and reports.
- Rate limits and text rules are a floor, not a guarantee. Human review is
  the safety layer.

## Reports and moderation

- People can report a message, a whole Hushspace (the last 20 messages are
  captured) or a person. They can block at the same time.
- Evidence is copied when the report is made, so deleting a message
  afterwards doesn't erase it. The reported person isn't told who reported.
- The queue puts urgent categories first. A report moves through *open, in
  review, then actioned, no action or escalated*, with notes and an assigned
  reviewer.
- Account states are *active*, *restricted* (can't invite or be found),
  *suspended* (read, export and delete only) and *removed*. Every moderator
  action needs a reason and is audited, as is every report view.
- **Still to build:** the moderator interface, appeals (a submission route
  for restricted or suspended users and a second-reviewer decision), and
  notifications to reporters about outcomes.

## Retention (proposed, for decision)

| Data | Proposal |
|---|---|
| Messages in open Hushspaces | Kept until either person deletes their identity (their own messages are then cleared). Each person can remove their own messages at any time |
| Closed Hushspaces | Readable for 30 days (so people can report), then purged |
| Reports and evidence | 12 months after closure, longer only where the law requires. Then deleted |
| Audit log | 24 months |
| Deleted identity | Profile, handle, code and blocks removed straight away, sent messages cleared, sessions ended. Reports kept as above |

## What we tell people (and never overclaim)

- It's private to the two of you, but **not end-to-end encrypted**, and
  reported messages are read by trained people.
- Pseudonyms aren't perfect anonymity. What you write can identify you.
- Screenshots can't be prevented.
- Hushmates are peers, not crisis responders or clinicians. Nothing is
  monitored in real time. Emergency numbers and Find a Helpline are always
  one tap away.
- No guarantee that a connection will reduce loneliness or improve mental
  health.

## Never

No AI in messaging, matching, moderation or summaries. No read receipts,
typing indicators or "last seen" by default. No streaks, guilt prompts or
re-engagement nudges. No advertising use or analytics of message content.
Blocking, reporting, privacy and safety controls are never behind a paywall,
and there are no message limits designed to push anyone to pay.
