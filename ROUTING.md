# Routing Contract

The spreadsheet remains the source of truth. Production routing must respect these data-layer rules:

1. Safety override and specific Support Trigger matching happen before normal rituals.
2. `supportFirstBypass=true` means render SUPPORT FIRST immediately.
3. Severe substance risk routes to SUPPORT FIRST.
4. Generic craving/urge terms favor Tier 1.
5. Habit/cue language favors Tier 2.
6. Tier 3 is only eligible when deliberation language is present.
7. During acute distress, LOW cognitive-load matches outrank MEDIUM/HIGH.
8. `Customer Display Alias` is the UI title. `Canonical Technique` is internal provenance.
9. Exclude previously used ritual IDs when possible before selecting a repeat.
